There are thousands of photos of my daughter on my phone. Some of them are beautiful. Most of them I will never see again. They sit in a camera roll between screenshots and receipts, backed up to a cloud I pay for every month, sorted by nothing more meaningful than the date the file was saved.

That's the problem we set out to fix with **Small Forever**, the first product made by Neex Creative in Dublin. It's a **personalised gift** for parents, grandparents and godparents: a child's photos, videos and voice, gathered into one private timeline and delivered on a keepsake drive.

## The problem isn't storage. It's looking.

Parents today aren't short of photos. We're short of a place to look at them. The cloud keeps everything, but it hands it back as an endless grid. Nobody sits down on a Sunday evening to scroll through 2024 in thumbnails.

What we wanted was closer to a family album: something you open on purpose, where the story is already in order, and where every moment carries a small note that makes it mean something. *Two years, eleven months and seven days.* The first time she said "Dada." The Christmas she understood the presents.

## What Small Forever is

Each Small Forever is built by hand, for one child. You send us the photos, videos and voice notes that matter. We put them in order, add the child's age to every moment, and build it into a single timeline. Then we deliver it on a drive, in a gift box, sealed with a wax letter.

There's nothing to install. You plug the drive into any computer, open it, and type the family password. It works offline, with no app, no account and no subscription. If our company disappeared tomorrow, the timeline would still open. That was a deliberate design choice: a keepsake shouldn't depend on someone else's server.

## The part that made us build it: letters to the future

The idea that turned this from a nice project into a product was the letters.

Parents write to their child now, and each letter stays sealed inside the timeline until a date they choose. One for their first day at school. One for their eighteenth birthday. When the child opens Small Forever years from now, they'll find a message from their parents, written when they were small, waiting for exactly that day.

There's also a printed letter in the box, sealed with wax and marked *do not open until*. It's the part people film when they unbox it.

## Being honest about "forever"

We thought hard about the name. Any single drive can fail, and we'd rather say that plainly than promise something no piece of hardware can keep. So every Small Forever comes with a simple 3-2-1 backup guide: three copies, in two places, with one outside the home. The Legacy plan includes a second copy for the grandparents, and you can add one to any plan.

"Forever" isn't something a drive gives you. It's a habit, and we make that habit easy.
